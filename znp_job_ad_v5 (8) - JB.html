Job description

Summary of role

We are looking for a Data Scientist / Sr. Data Scientist for our Analytics team, making significant contributions in designing analytical / data-science projects involving Machine Learning / Deep Learning models, Statistical / AI modelling skills, etc.

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Ability to work with large data sets and present conclusions to key stakeholders; data management using Python / Spark
  • Working through the phases of project
  • Define data requirements for creating a model and understand the business problem
  • Optimise processing speed, automate iterative tasks, write codes in modular form with high reusability
  • Clean, aggregate, analyse, interpret data, and carry out quality analysis
  • Development of AI / ML models or statistical / econometric models
  • Hands-on in Market Mix Optimisation, Pricing and Promotion Optimisation, Assortment optimisation

Educational Background / Qualifications

  • BTech in Computer Science, MCA or Master's degree in Statistical Econometrics / Mathematics or related subjects
  • MPhi / PhD in Statistical Econometrics or related fields (preferred)
